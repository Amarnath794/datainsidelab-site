Power BI Project

Olist E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end ecommerce analytics dashboard built using Power BI to analyze revenue, customer behavior, geographic performance, and business growth trends.

View GitHub Profile Explore Project Repository

Project overview

This project was developed using the Olist Brazilian ecommerce dataset to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights. The dashboard focuses on helping business users monitor sales performance, customer activity, product trends, and operational metrics through interactive Power BI reports.

The complete solution includes Power Query transformations, semantic modeling, DAX measures, KPI tracking, time intelligence calculations, and multiple analytical dashboards with interactive slicers and filters.

Advanced DAX measures and KPI calculations

One of the core strengths of this project is the semantic modeling and advanced DAX implementation used to build business KPIs, time intelligence reporting, revenue calculations, and customer analytics. 

Total Revenue =
SUM(order_items[price])

Completed Orders =
CALCULATE(
    DISTINCTCOUNT(orders[order_id]),
    orders[order_status] = "delivered"
)

AOV_Delivered =
DIVIDE([Revenue_Delivered], [Completed Orders])

Sales % of Total =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Revenue],
    CALCULATE([Total Revenue], ALL(products))
)

Revenue MOM Growth % =
VAR PreviousMonthRevenue =
    CALCULATE(
        [Total Revenue],
        DATEADD(calendar[date], -1, MONTH)
    )
RETURN
DIVIDE(
    [Total Revenue] - PreviousMonthRevenue,
    PreviousMonthRevenue
)

Revenue MoM Safe =
IF(
    ISBLANK([Revenue MOM Growth %]),
    0,
    [Revenue MOM Growth %]
)

Monthly Revenue =
CALCULATE(
    [Total Revenue],
    DATESMTD(calendar[date])
)

The dashboard uses advanced DAX functions including CALCULATE, DATEADD, ALL, VAR, RETURN, DIVIDE, ISBLANK, and DISTINCTCOUNT to build scalable analytical measures and dynamic business reporting.

Time intelligence calculations were implemented to analyze Month-over-Month growth, moving averages, revenue trends, customer behavior, and category contribution analysis.

Revenue analysis dashboard

This dashboard section focuses on revenue performance, monthly sales trends, category contribution, and payment insights. It helps identify revenue growth opportunities and high-performing business segments.

Orders and customer analytics

The customer analytics dashboard highlights customer behavior, repeat purchases, order distribution, and engagement patterns. The analysis helps understand customer retention and business growth performance.

Geographic insights

Geographic analysis provides insights into state-wise revenue, regional demand, and delivery performance. These insights can support market expansion and operational optimization decisions.

Time intelligence analysis

Time-based analysis was implemented using DAX measures and moving average calculations to track monthly performance, seasonal trends, and business growth patterns over time.

Business recommendations

The final dashboard summarizes business recommendations and strategic insights derived from the analysis. These recommendations help stakeholders identify improvement areas and make data-driven decisions.

Data model and semantic design

The project includes a structured Power BI semantic model with multiple related tables for orders, customers, products, sellers, and payments. Relationships and DAX calculations were designed to support efficient reporting and accurate KPI calculations.

Key business insights

  • Some product categories generated significantly higher revenue compared to others.
  • Repeat customers contributed strongly to overall business performance.
  • Revenue distribution varied heavily across geographic regions.
  • Delivery performance influenced customer satisfaction trends.
  • Monthly sales analysis revealed seasonal growth patterns.
