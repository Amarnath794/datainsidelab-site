Project goal

The goal of this project is to turn ecommerce data into a dashboard that helps users understand business performance. A good dashboard should make it easy to answer questions about sales, profit, customers, product categories, and trends over time.

Business questions

  • Which products and categories perform best?
  • How are sales changing over time?
  • Which customer or region segments need attention?
  • Where can the business improve revenue or profitability?
